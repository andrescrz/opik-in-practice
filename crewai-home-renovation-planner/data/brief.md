# Client brief: Maple Court, unit 4B (fictional)

Owners: the Okonkwo-Reyes household. Two-bedroom condo, built 1994.

## Goals
- Kitchen: replace the galley layout's cabinets and counters, keep the plumbing
  where it is, add under-cabinet lighting and a new range hood.
- Bathroom (the only full bath): replace the tub with a walk-in shower, new
  vanity, new floor tile, new exhaust fan.

## Constraints
- Total budget ceiling: 38,000 USD including a 10% contingency.
- The condo board allows contractor work Monday to Friday, 9:00-16:00 only.
- Elevator reservations must be booked 5 business days ahead for each delivery.
- The household has one bathroom, so the bath must not be out of service for
  more than 10 consecutive days.
- Owners travel from 2 March to 15 March and want the noisy demolition done
  inside that window.

## Preferences
- Quartz counters, shaker-style cabinets, large-format porcelain tile.
- Would trade a fancier faucet for a better vanity.
