# Campgrounds

## Fernwater Campground
Fernwater has 42 drive-in sites with fire rings, picnic tables and flush toilets.
Sites cost 28 dollars per night and can be reserved up to six months ahead.
Check-in is after 2 pm and check-out is by 11 am. Quiet hours run from 10 pm
to 6 am.

## Cinder Flat walk-in sites
Cinder Flat has 8 walk-in sites reached by a 1.5 km track from the Cinder Pass
parking lot. There is a pit toilet and no drinking water, so bring or filter
your own. Sites cost 15 dollars per night and are first come, first served.

## Campfire rules
Campfires are only allowed in the metal fire rings at Fernwater. Cinder Flat is
stove-only. A total fire ban is announced at the Visitor Centre when the fire
danger rating reaches Extreme, and it covers every campground in the park.
Firewood must be bought locally; do not bring it from outside the park.

## Checking in late
Arrivals after 9 pm should use the lit self check-in board at the Fernwater
entrance, where the site number is written next to the reservation name. The
campground host is on duty from 8 am to 8 pm.
