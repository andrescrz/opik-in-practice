# Wildlife and safety

## Bear safety
Larkspur Hollow is home to black bears. Store all food, rubbish and toiletries
in the bear lockers provided at campgrounds or in a hard-sided vehicle. Carry bear
spray on every trail above the Fernwater valley. Keep at least 100 metres from a
bear; if one approaches, stay calm, back away slowly and never run.

## Elk and other large animals
Keep 25 metres from elk, deer and moose. During the autumn rut from mid-September
to late October, bull elk can be aggressive near the Hollow Gate meadows. Never
feed wildlife; fines start at 250 dollars.

## Drones and wildlife
Flying drones is prohibited throughout the park. Rangers may confiscate drones
flown near nesting sites, and visitors can be fined.

## Emergencies and phone coverage
There is no mobile coverage beyond the Visitor Centre. In an emergency, use the
yellow ranger phones at trailheads or campground entrances, or press the SOS
button on a satellite messenger. Rangers respond to ranger-phone calls around the
clock.
