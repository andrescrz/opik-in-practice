# Permits and fees

## Day-use entry
Day-use entry is 12 dollars per vehicle and valid for three consecutive days.
Visitors arriving on foot or by bicycle pay 4 dollars per person. Entry fees are
paid at the Hollow Gate kiosk or on the park's website.

## Overnight backcountry permits
Anyone sleeping outside a numbered campground needs a backcountry permit. Permits
cost 9 dollars per person per night and are limited to 20 hikers per night on the
Cinder Pass route. Reservations open 60 days before the trip date and cannot be
changed within 48 hours of departure.

## Annual pass
The Hollow Annual Pass costs 55 dollars and covers day-use entry for one vehicle
for twelve months from the month of purchase. It does not cover campground fees
or backcountry permits.

## Fishing licences
Fishing in Mossglass Lake requires a park fishing licence at 6 dollars per day.
Only catch-and-release is allowed, and barbless hooks are mandatory. Children
under 12 fish free when with a licensed adult.
