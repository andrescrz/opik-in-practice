# Trails of Larkspur Hollow National Park

## Ember Ridge Trail
Ember Ridge is a 9.4 km loop that climbs 610 m through burnt-pine forest to a
fire lookout. Allow five to six hours. The trail is exposed above the treeline,
so turn back if thunderstorms are forecast. Dogs are not allowed on Ember Ridge
because of ground-nesting birds.

## Mossglass Lake Walk
The Mossglass Lake Walk is a flat 3 km boardwalk circuit and the only trail in
the park that is fully wheelchair and stroller accessible. It starts at the
Visitor Centre and takes about 50 minutes. Leashed dogs are welcome.

## Trail closures and seasons
Higher trails, including Ember Ridge and the Cinder Pass route, are usually
snow-free from 1 July to 30 September. The Cinder Pass route closes on 1 October
each year regardless of conditions. Closure notices are posted at the Visitor
Centre and at every trailhead board.

## Trail difficulty ratings
Trails are rated Easy, Moderate or Strenuous. Easy trails are under 5 km with
less than 100 m of climbing. Strenuous trails gain more than 500 m. Ember Ridge
is rated Strenuous; Mossglass Lake Walk is rated Easy.
