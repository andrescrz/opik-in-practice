# Watering guide

General rule: one deep soak beats frequent sprinkles. Roots follow the water, so shallow watering gives shallow roots.

- Check the soil: push a finger 5 cm in. If it is dry at that depth, water.
- Best time is early morning; evening watering helps slugs and mildew.
- Vegetable beds in a dry fortnight: about 10 litres per square metre per week, in one or two soaks.
- Containers dry out fast: in summer check daily, and water until it runs out of the drainage holes.
- Newly planted shrubs and trees: 10 litres every week for the first summer.
- Mulch 5 cm deep with compost or leaf mould after watering to cut evaporation by about half.
- Lawn: Fernwick Vale lawns green up on their own after autumn rain; do not water established lawns.
- Beans and courgettes need steady water when flowering; peas need it when pods are filling.
