# Planting calendar for Fernwick Vale (cool-temperate, last frost around 12 May, first frost around 20 October)

Fernwick Vale is a fictional valley garden district. All dates are approximate and shift by about a week in cold springs.

- Early March: start leeks, onions and celery indoors under lights.
- Early April: sow peas, spinach and radishes outdoors; start tomatoes and peppers indoors.
- Late April: harden off brassica seedlings, plant out potatoes.
- Mid May (after the 12 May frost date): plant out tomatoes, peppers, courgettes and runner beans. Sow basil outdoors only once nights stay above 10 C.
- June: succession-sow lettuce every two weeks; sow carrots and beetroot.
- Early August: sow autumn spinach, kale and winter radish.
- September: plant garlic cloves in the first week of October for midsummer harvest; sow green manure (field beans) on bare beds.
- November: mulch perennial beds with leaf mould and move tender pots under cover.
