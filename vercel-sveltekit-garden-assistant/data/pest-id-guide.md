# Pest identification guide (Fernwick Vale plot)

Aphids: clusters of tiny green, black or grey insects on new shoots and under leaves, with sticky honeydew and curled leaves. Fix: squash by hand or blast with water; encourage ladybirds and hoverflies; a spray of dilute soft soap works on heavy infestations. Avoid high-nitrogen feed which makes soft growth they love.

Slugs and snails: ragged holes in leaves, silvery trails, most damage at night and after rain. Fix: evening torch patrols, beer traps, copper tape on pots, and keep seedlings covered until they are sturdy.

Cabbage white caterpillars: green caterpillars and yellow egg clusters under brassica leaves, leaves reduced to ribs. Fix: fine mesh over the crop from planting, and pick off eggs weekly in July and August.

Carrot fly: carrots with rusty tunnels and wilting foliage. Fix: sow thinly, thin and harvest in the evening, and fence the bed with a 60 cm high barrier of fine mesh.

Powdery mildew: white dusty coating on courgette and squash leaves in late summer. Fix: water at the roots, improve air flow, remove worst leaves. It rarely affects the harvest.
