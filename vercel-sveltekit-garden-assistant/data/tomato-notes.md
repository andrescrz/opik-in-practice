# Tomatoes: 'Lanternfruit' cordon variety and bush types

Tomato notes from the Fernwick Vale community plot.

Planting: set young plants deep, burying the stem up to the first true leaves so extra roots form. Space cordon types 50 cm apart, bush types 70 cm. Plant out after 12 May once nights hold above 8 C.

Watering: water deeply twice a week rather than a little every day. Aim for about 4 litres per plant per watering in warm weather. Irregular watering causes split fruit and blossom end rot (a dark sunken patch at the base of the fruit).

Feeding: switch to a high-potash feed once the first truss has set fruit.

Common problems: yellow lower leaves late in summer are normal ageing; yellow upper leaves with curling suggests overfeeding. Brown patches with a yellow halo on leaves and a dry, ring-marked spot on stems point to early blight: remove affected leaves and keep foliage dry.
