# Soil and compost

Fernwick Vale soil is heavy clay loam, slightly acidic (pH around 6.3). Dig in compost each autumn, but avoid walking on wet beds.

Compost: balance green material (kitchen scraps, grass clippings) with brown (cardboard, dry leaves) at about one to one. Turn monthly. Finished compost is dark, crumbly and smells earthy, usually ready in six to nine months.

Raised beds: 25 cm deep is plenty for most vegetables; fill with two parts topsoil to one part compost.

Green manure: sow field beans in October on empty beds; chop them down in March and leave them to rot on the surface for two weeks before planting.
