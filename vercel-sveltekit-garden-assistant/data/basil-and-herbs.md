# Basil and soft herbs

Basil ('Genovese Brightleaf' in the community seed swap) hates cold. Sow indoors in April, plant out in late May, or sow directly in June. Pinch the top pair of leaves off once a plant has six leaves so it bushes out instead of flowering.

Watering: water at the base in the morning, about 250 ml per pot plant every other day in summer. Wet leaves overnight invite downy mildew (yellow patches above, grey fuzz underneath).

Parsley and coriander prefer part shade and bolt in heat; sow a new row every four weeks from April to August.

Mint should always be grown in a buried pot because it spreads aggressively by runners.
