# Lanternfall — rulebook (2-4 players, about 30 minutes)

## §1.1 Goal
Players are lamplighters on a ring of six canal tiles. The first player to
reach 12 glow points wins the game immediately.

## §1.2 Setup
Each player starts with 5 lantern cards and 2 oil tokens, and places a boat
on any canal tile. The youngest player goes first.

## §2.1 Turn structure
A turn has three phases in order: Draw, Float, Light. Play then passes
clockwise.

## §2.2 Draw
Draw 2 cards from the deck. The hand limit is 7 cards: at the end of your
turn, discard down to 7.

## §2.3 Float
Move your boat up to 3 tiles along the canal in either direction. Passing
over a bridge tile costs 1 oil token.

## §3.1 Lighting
Play a lantern card whose color matches the tile you stopped on to light
it and score 2 glow points. You may light only one tile per turn unless you
play a Festival card, which allows a second.

## §3.2 Wind die
At the start of the Light phase, roll the wind die. On a 1, one of your lit
lanterns goes out and you lose 1 glow point. Any other result does nothing.

## §3.3 Refilling oil
Instead of drawing in the Draw phase, you may take 3 oil tokens. You can
never hold more than 6 oil tokens.

## §4.1 Trading
Once per turn you may swap one card with another player whose boat shares
your tile. Oil tokens can never be traded.

## §4.2 Fog cards
A Fog card may be played during an opponent's Float phase to cancel their
movement for that turn. Playing it costs you 1 oil token.

## §4.3 Tiebreaks
If two players reach 12 glow points in the same round, the player with the
most oil tokens left wins. If oil is also tied, the victory is shared.
