# Recipes of the Copperkettle Brewers

## House Amber Ale
The club's flagship batch is 20 litres. Mash 4 kg of pale malt and 400 g of crystal malt at 67 C for 60 minutes. Boil for 60 minutes with 30 g of Fuggle hops at the start and 15 g at flameout. Pitch one packet of the club's standard ale yeast once the wort is below 22 C.

## Ginger Pear Cider
Press 12 kg of pears into a 20 litre carboy and add 80 g of fresh grated ginger. Pitch cider yeast and ferment for three weeks. Back-sweetening is forbidden before bottling because it restarts fermentation and bottles can burst.

## Winter Stout
Mash 5 kg of pale malt with 500 g of roasted barley at 68 C. Add 200 g of lactose in the last 10 minutes of the boil for body. The stout needs six weeks of bottle conditioning before it may be poured at a club night.
