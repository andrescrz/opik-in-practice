# Sanitation Rules

## Cleaning Before Sanitising
Everything that touches wort or beer after the boil must be cleaned first with the club's oxygen cleaner and then sanitised. Sanitiser does nothing on a dirty surface, so a rinse-only fermenter counts as unsanitised.

## Sanitiser Contact Time
Use the no-rinse acid sanitiser at 5 ml per 5 litres of water. Surfaces need two full minutes of wet contact. Foam left in the bucket is fine and must not be rinsed away.

## Cleaning Day
Cleaning day is the first Saturday of each month at 10:00. Every member who borrowed equipment that month must attend, and the clubhouse keys are returned to the treasurer afterwards.
