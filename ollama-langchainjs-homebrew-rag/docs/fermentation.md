# Fermentation Temperatures

## Ale Fermentation Range
Ales ferment between 18 and 21 C. Above 24 C the yeast produces fusel alcohols that taste like nail polish remover, and the batch is marked as a training batch rather than served.

## Lager Fermentation Range
Lagers ferment at 9 to 12 C in the cellar fridge. After the primary fermentation finishes, raise the temperature to 16 C for two days for a diacetyl rest, then lower it by 2 C per day to 2 C for lagering.

## Cider Fermentation Range
Ciders ferment at 15 to 18 C in the shed, which stays cool all year. The shed thermometer is checked by whoever opens the shed first each morning, and readings go in the logbook.
