# Equipment Lending Rules

## Borrowing Limits
Members in good standing may borrow one item at a time for up to 14 days. The 30 litre kettle, the wort chiller and the hydrometer set are the most requested items, so reservations are made on the clubhouse whiteboard.

## Deposits
The bottle capper and the grain mill require a 25 euro deposit, returned when the item is handed back clean. The kettle requires a 40 euro deposit because the burner element has been replaced twice.

## Late and Damaged Returns
A late return costs 2 euro per day, and the borrower is suspended from lending after 5 late days. Damaged equipment must be reported to the treasurer in writing within two days, and the borrower pays for repair at cost.
